## Brand & Style
This design system defines an operational flight-deck interface engineered for extravehicular and station-board physiological survival. The aesthetic merges NASA flight-director rigor with a contemporary mobile-first heads-up display (HUD). 

### Visual Tenets
- **Mission Critical Precision:** Zero ornamental clutter. Every pixel, line width, and token communicates urgency, status, or structural boundary.
- **Deep Space HUD:** Deep space obsidian containers suspended with low-attenuation cyan borders, high-contrast luminous data clusters, and dedicated alert lanes.
- **Tactile Emergency Ergonomics:** Heavy touch targets configured for gloved, microgravity, or high-vibration interactions; rapid scan patterns prioritization for life-support vitals (SpO2, heart rate variability, radiation flux, core temp).
- **Data Provenance Integrity:** Absolute visual separation between validated historical archive feeds and live simulated contingency runs.

## Colors
The palette is calibrated strictly for high-contrast OLED legibility in low-light orbital habitats and direct-sun visor glare.

- **Primary (`#00F0FF`):** Flight vector cyan. Used for vital borders, interactive state rings, primary control highlights, and active telemetry channels.
- **Secondary (`#00FF66`):** Nominal telemetry green. Denotes stable biometric states, pressurized life support, and verified telemetry locks.
- **Tertiary (`#FFB703`):** Advisory amber. Communicates drift, sensor degradation, and non-critical physiological alerts.
- **Critical Alert (`#FF2A55`):** Mission critical red. Reserved exclusively for hypoxic warnings, rapid cabin decompression, tachycardia/arrhythmia, and manual abort triggers.
- **Void Bases (`#030712`, `#070D1D`, `#0B132B`):** Multi-tiered spatial canvas structure. Deep obsidian provides limitless infinite contrast, while midnight sub-layers separate secondary cards and status arrays.
- **Data Readout Neutrals (`#F8FAFC`, `#94A3B8`, `#475569`):** Pure optical white for real-time numeric readouts; muted slate for engineering metrics, labels, and coordinate timestamps.

## Typography
Typography is separated strictly into narrative, architectural headings, and monospaced telemetry readouts.

- **Headlines (Space Grotesk):** Provides an authoritative aerospace identity. Headlines must maintain uppercase tracking on status labels and module identifiers.
- **Body (Inter):** Maximizes readability for clinical observations, medical protocol procedures, and event log descriptions.
- **Labels, Telemetry, and Identifiers (JetBrains Mono):** Monospaced numeric tables ensure zero horizontal jitter during continuous real-time data streaming (e.g., heart rates, partial pressure graphs, crew IDs).

## Layout & Spacing
The layout adheres to a strict 4-column dynamic grid on mobile devices, transitioning to an 8-column layout on telemetry tablets and a 12-column layout on mission-control desktop viewports.

- **Mobile Viewport Optimization:** Margins are set to `1rem` to maximize actionable surface area on small displays. Key biometric cards use tight internal `space-md` gaps to retain maximum data density above the fold.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Monospaced data groups employ compact `space-xs` and `space-sm` increments to keep related indicators (e.g., label, value, unit) grouped into clear scan units.
- **Reflow & Safety Insets:** Primary controls, contingency triggers, and critical acknowledge banners are anchored within standard thumb zones and respect device safe areas with `space-lg` bottom padding.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation is achieved using translucent glassmorphism paired with luminous boundary radiance rather than drop shadows.

- **Void Surface Base:** Background sits at `#030712`.
- **Card Tier 1 (Telemetry Panels):** Surface `#070D1D` with 80% opacity and an active `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`. Outlined with a 1px solid border of `#00F0FF` at 18% opacity.
- **Card Tier 2 (Selected / Active HUD):** Surface `#0B132B` at 90% opacity, bordered by 1px solid `#00F0FF` with a cyan outer glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 12px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.25)`.
- **Critical Emergency Tier:** Surface `#18040A` at 95% opacity, flanked by a 1.5px solid border of `#FF2A55` and a dynamic perimeter beacon: `box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(255, 42, 85, 0.40)`.

## Shapes
Geometry leans technical, industrial, and compact. 

- **Corner Radii:** Set to soft angularity (`roundedness: 1`), keeping standard modules at `0.25rem` (4px) to retain an instrumental instrument-panel feel.
- **Card Containers:** Standardize at `0.5rem` (`rounded-lg`) to balance structural rigour with thumb-friendly tactility.
- **Status Badges & Pills:** Retain clipped capsule contours (`rounded-full`) to immediately contrast with rectangular data monitors.

## Components

### 1. Control Buttons
- **Primary Flight Action:** Solid `#00F0FF` fill with `#030712` bold typography. Height: 48px minimum. Soft 4px outer edge. Active state provides an inward micro-glow.
- **Critical Abort / Contingency:** Solid `#FF2A55` background, high-contrast white monospaced text, reinforced with diagonal hazard striping accents on the container edge.
- **Sub-System Ghost:** Transparent background with a 1px `#38BDF8` border at 40% opacity, transitioning to 100% opacity on press.

### 2. Telemetry Cards
- Encased in glassmorphic midnight panels (`#070D1D` at 80% with cyan wire border).
- Structured in three vertical zones: Micro Header (parameter name + status dot), Value Anchor (large monospaced numerical readout + engineering unit), and Sparkline/Min-Max trajectory bounds.

### 3. Data Provenance Badges
- **NASA OSDR Archive:** Crisp background tint (`rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.12)`), text `#00F0FF`, border 1px solid `#00F0FF`, uppercase monospaced text prefixed with a geometric archival node glyph `[OSDR-FLT]`.
- **SIMULATED / DEMO:** Crisp warning background (`rgba(255, 183, 3, 0.12)`), text `#FFB703`, border 1px dashed `#FFB703`, uppercase label `[SIM-RUN]`.

### 4. Status Indicator Pills
- **Nominal:** 6px solid `#00FF66` beacon with `0 0 6px #00FF66` radial glow, accompanying `#00FF66` text over `#022413`.
- **Advisory:** Pulsing `#FFB703` indicator over `#261A02`.
- **Emergency:** Rapid flashing `#FF2A55` indicator over `#2B050E`.

### 5. Input Fields & Selectors
- Technical input surfaces utilizing `#070D1D` base, 1px `#475569` border, and cyan focus state (`#00F0FF`). Monospaced text input with uppercase baseline assistance.

### 6. Emergency Mission Banner
- Full-bleed sticky alert strip positioned at top/bottom of screen. Background `#FF2A55`, black text (`#030712`), prominent audio-mute toggle, countdown clearance timer, and tactile 'ACKNOWLEDGE' swipe slider.
